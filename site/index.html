About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at National Tsing Hua University, advised by Prof. Chun-Yao Wang and Prof. Bei Yu at The Chinese University of Hong Kong.

My research focuses on electronic design automation (EDA). I work on learning and optimization methods for circuit design, with interests in LLMs for EDA, logic synthesis and RTL recovery, and physical design automation.

I received my M.S. in Computer Science from National Tsing Hua University in 2023 and my B.S. in Computer Science and Information Engineering from Ming Chuan University in 2021.
